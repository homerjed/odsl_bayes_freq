### References

- [Statistical Signal Processing](https://www.ece.rice.edu/~dhj/courses/elec531/notes.pdf) — Don H. Johnson (2013); useful chapter on estimation theory (Chapter 4).
- [The Probability of the Cosmos: Classical, Quantum, Bayesian](https://www.usm.lmu.de/~oliverf/files/probability_of_the_cosmos.pdf) — Oliver Friedrich and Jed Homer.
- Dodelson–Schneider effect: [The Effect of Covariance Estimator Error on Cosmological Parameter Constraints](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.2593) — Scott Dodelson and Michael D. Schneider (2013).
- Frequentist matching prior: [Matching Bayesian and frequentist coverage probabilities when using an approximate data covariance matrix](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10402) — Will J. Percival, Oliver Friedrich, Elena Sellentin, and Alan Heavens (2021 preprint).
- [Probabilistic Machine Learning book series](https://probml.github.io/pml-book/) — Kevin P. Murphy; excellent textbooks for statistics and machine learning.
- [Bayes in the sky: Bayesian inference and model selection in cosmology](https://arxiv.org/abs/0803.4089) — Roberto Trotta (2008); introduction to Bayesian probability, inference, and model selection.
- [A Tutorial on Fisher Information](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1705.01064) — Alexander Ly, Maarten Marsman, Josine Verhagen, Raoul Grasman, and Eric-Jan Wagenmakers (2017); Fisher information in frequentist inference, Bayesian priors, and model complexity.
